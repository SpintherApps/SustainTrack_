Privacy Policy

SustainTrack — the app for runners

Last updated: October 18, 2026 · Version 3

This policy explains what information SustainTrack ("the app", "we") handles when you use it, why, and what you can do about it. SustainTrack is published by Spintherlight. We collect as little as we can: the app does not show ads, does not sell your data and does not include advertising or analytics trackers.

1. Information We Collect

A device identifier

The first time you open the app, it creates a user for your device. That user is tied to an identifier of the device: on Android, the system's Android ID; on iPhone and iPad, a random identifier that the app stores in your Keychain (and that iCloud Keychain may share between your own Apple devices); on Mac, a random identifier stored on the computer. We also keep the platform (Android, iOS or desktop) and the date and time of the first and the last time the app connected.

Your account, if you sign in

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google or Apple, our authentication provider receives from them an account identifier, your email address and, for Google, the address of your profile picture. We do not store or read your name. We show your picture inside the app; we never show your email to other people.

Content you save

  • Saved maps: the place, the area of the map and a small picture (thumbnail) of it.
  • My car: the position where you parked, and the photo and note you add. It is stored only on your device.

The social network

If you create a profile, we store on our servers what you choose to share so that other people can see it: your username, the name you choose to show, your profile text and picture, your posts and their photos, your likes and comments, who you follow and who follows you, follow requests, the people you block, the groups you join and the notifications about all of this.

  • Your profile is public by default. You can make it private in your profile: then only the people you approve can see your posts and lists. People you block cannot see you or interact with you.
  • What you post in a group is seen only by the people that group allows.
  • Direct messages are currently stored only on your device.
  • You can edit or delete your posts at any time.

Your date of birth

The social network is only for adults. To use it, we ask for your full date of birth once and use it to check that you meet the minimum age: 18, or older where the law of your country requires it.

  • Who sees it: only our servers. It is stored separately from your profile, it is not public, other people cannot see it, and the app does not show it to you again or keep it on your phone. The only thing that leaves our servers is whether you meet the minimum age.
  • No date, no social network: if you do not give it, or if you do not meet the minimum age, you cannot see or use the social network (profiles, posts, stories, messages and groups). The rest of the app works the same.
  • Declaring is not verifying: for now we rely on the date you give. In the future we may ask for an age check through a specialised provider; if we do, we will tell you first and update this policy.
  • Correcting it: the date cannot be changed in the app. If you made a mistake, write to us (see "Contact Us") and we will correct it after checking it.
  • Country: the minimum age may depend on the country you connect from, which our servers determine from the connection, not from your phone's settings.

Location

The app asks for your location only when a feature needs it (your position on the map, places near you). Your location is used on your device and is sent to our services only to answer that request; we do not keep a history of where you have been. "My car" reads your position only while its screen is open, and keeps it on your device. To show the weather, the app sends an approximate position (or lets the weather service estimate it from your connection) without asking for the location permission.

Races and their official course

The race calendar shows public information published by the organisers (dates, place, distances, website and their public contact details). Race pictures are loaded from the organiser's website. When you tap "Official GPX", your device downloads the course file directly from the organiser's website (which, like any website, receives your IP address) and draws it on the map. The file is kept only in the app's memory while you look at it: it is not sent to us and it is not saved on our servers or on your device. The contact buttons (Instagram, Facebook, call, email) open those apps or services, which have their own privacy policies.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, health data, advertising identifiers or browsing activity. The app accesses your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo, and we only receive the photos you decide to post. The app has a feature to collect technical statistics about the device (model, system version, language); it is turned off and will only be turned on with your consent and an update to this policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To keep your saved maps and settings and give them back to you when you reinstall the app or change phones.
  • To let you sign in and to recognise your account on your devices.
  • To show maps, places to run, races and the weather for where you are.
  • To show what you share in the social network to the people allowed to see it, and to notify you of follows, likes, comments and group activity inside the app.
  • To protect the service against abuse and to fix errors.
  • To check that you meet the minimum age to use the social network. Your date of birth is not used for advertising, to build profiles of you or to recommend content.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not build profiles of you.

4. Minors

The social network is only for people aged 18 or over (or the higher minimum age set by the law of your country). We do not allow younger people to use it and we do not knowingly collect their data in it.

Reports. If you think an account belongs to a minor, you can report it from its profile. A person on our team reviews every report: a report never suspends an account on its own, and we never decide by automatic inference. The reported person does not learn who reported them, and the person who reports does not receive the outcome.

If we confirm that an account belongs to a minor, we suspend it: it loses access to the social network and nobody can see it. We keep its data for 30 days so it can appeal (write to us, see "Contact Us"). After that period we delete its social network data.

If someone gives a date that does not meet the minimum age, we keep that date only so that a different one cannot be given later, and we do not use their data for anything else.

The rest of SustainTrack is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age set by the law of your country if it is higher, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data Security

Connections between the app and our services are encrypted. Your session is stored in the secure storage of your device (Keychain on Apple devices, Android Keystore on Android). Access to the database is restricted so that each user can read and change only their own data. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw your consent. We do not sell personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us (see below). You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Why we are allowed to process your data

  • To provide the service you asked for: your device user, your account, your saved content and what you share in the social network.
  • Your consent: location, and any optional statistics if we enable them.
  • Our legitimate interest: keeping the service secure and working.
  • Your date of birth: it is necessary to provide the social network you asked for, which is only for adults: without it we cannot check that you meet the minimum age.

Where your data is processed

Our database is hosted in the United States. Cloudflare serves maps and files from data centres around the world. If you use the app from another country, your data is transferred to those locations.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data while you use the app. Device users without an account and without saved content may be deleted after a period of inactivity. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your data from our systems within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep.

Your date of birth is kept while your account exists and is deleted with it. If an account is confirmed to belong to a minor, its social network data is kept for 30 days for an appeal and then deleted. Of that account we keep only the record that it was confirmed as a minor (without the date of birth) and of the reports that were reviewed (without their text), to prevent it from registering again with another date and to be able to answer a complaint.

7. Account Deletion

You can sign out at any time from your profile, and delete your posts whenever you want. To delete your account and the data linked to it (including your social profile, posts and photos), write to us from the email address of your account, or tell us the sign-in method you use. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device. When you delete your account we also delete your date of birth.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy we will update the date and the version at the top of this page, and the app will ask you to review it again when the change is significant.
